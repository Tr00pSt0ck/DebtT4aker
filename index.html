Privacy

Your data never leaves.

🔒
No Server. Ever.
There is no backend. No API. No database. The app is a single HTML file that runs entirely in your browser.
📦
localStorage Only
Every debt record is stored in your browser's localStorage under your full control. Export it, import it, clear it — your choice.
🌐
Works Fully Offline
After the first load, zero internet required. Plane mode, dead zone, roaming — makes no difference. Your tracker is always there.
